# Attestation language — the drafting template for counsel (not legal advice)

The mainnet-verdict legal gate is not a research problem any more; it has a template.
Hand counsel these three anchors and this draft language.

## The three anchors for the brief
1. **SEC Staff "Statement on Tokenized Securities" (Jan 28, 2026)** + Atkins' Nov-2025
   Token Taxonomy: "securities, however represented, remain securities… economic reality
   trumps labels." Critically, the Staff taxonomy explicitly recognizes securities
   tokenized **by third parties unaffiliated with the issuer** — directly relevant to our
   unsolicited/permissionless posture. (Staff-level, no legal force, but the map counsel
   will use.)
2. **Unsolicited-credit-rating disclaimer model** (S&P / Moody's) — the closest legal
   analog for publishing an unsolicited opinion on a named security, historically shielded
   by First Amendment opinion protection; SEC Rule 17g-5 even created a mechanism for
   NRSROs to issue *unsolicited* ratings.
3. **IOSCO Final Report on Tokenisation (Nov 11, 2025, IOSCOPD809)** — global risk framing;
   "same activity, same risk, same regulatory outcome."

## Draft attestation-language template (adapted from CRA disclaimers — counsel to finalize)
> This is an independent, cryptographically signed **statement of opinion / measurement**
> as of the date expressed. It is **not a statement of fact**, **not a credit rating**,
> **not investment advice**, **not a recommendation** to buy, hold, or sell any security,
> and does **not address the suitability** of any instrument for any person. Council of AI
> is **not a fiduciary or investment adviser**. The measurement is produced by a
> **deterministic, published methodology** and is **not solicited or paid for by any
> issuer**. It is attached **alongside** the referenced instrument and confers **no rights
> in, and no claim on,** that instrument. Represented value is distinguished from
> distributed value where applicable.

## The three questions counsel must resolve before mainnet publish
1. Does a published "verdict" on a named security constitute **investment advice / a
   recommendation** (Investment Advisers Act exposure)?
2. Does the **unsolicited/permissionless** nature create **tortious-interference or
   defamation** surface the opinion-framing must cover?
3. Do our outputs risk **NRSRO "credit rating"** characterization — and if so, is the
   non-issuer-paid + methodology-transparency posture sufficient, or is registration
   analysis needed before scaling in any jurisdiction?

## Standing hygiene (do regardless)
Never the word "certification" or "credit rating" on any surface. Always "compliance /
measurement attestation." Publish the methodology. Document, per asset, that no issuer
paid for the measurement. Carry an as-of date. Express represented≠distributed for any
instrument where they differ (esp. JMWH — negative-signal demonstration only, never an
endorsement).
