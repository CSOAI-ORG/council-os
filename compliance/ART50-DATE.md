# Article 50 — one date, from EUR-Lex

Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 Art. 113, consolidated with Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI, in force 27 July 2026). ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/2026-07-27

| Obligation | Applies from |
|---|---|
| **Art. 50(1), 50(3), 50(4)** transparency (interaction, deepfakes, public-interest text) | **2 August 2026** (not postponed) |
| **Art. 50(2)** machine-readable marking for systems **placed on the market from 2 Aug 2026** | **2 August 2026** |
| **Art. 50(2)** marking for generative systems **already on the market before 2 Aug 2026** | **2 December 2026** (Art. 50(2) + Art. 111 transitional) |

Do **not** cite 2 November 2026. That copy is a defect in our own READMEs.
